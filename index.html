Standing kitchen system — built around one Trader Joe's run

The Combo Plate

A stocked-fridge system for Mexican-inspired tacos, quesadillas, and protein bowls — pick one item per category, combine, eat. Leans hard on Trader Joe's marinated meats, precooked proteins, and frozen shortcuts to cut the actual cook time to almost nothing.

01

Three ways to combine it

Same ingredients, three formats depending on your mood and how much time you've got. None of these are recipes — they're assembly order.

Tacos ~6 min
  1. Warm tortillas (dry skillet, 20 sec/side)
  2. Seasoned protein in the center
  3. Handful of cabbage slaw
  4. Spoon of pico, squeeze of lime
  5. Cheese or cotija on top
~5–6oz protein → 35–45g protein
Quesadillas ~10 min
  1. Cheese on half a flour tortilla
  2. Protein + sautéed pepper/onion
  3. Fold, press into dry skillet
  4. 2–3 min a side until cheese sets
  5. Dip in salsa or Greek yogurt
~5–6oz protein + cheese → 45–55g protein
Protein bowls ~10 min
  1. Rice or greens as the base
  2. Protein, then black beans
  3. Corn, pico or salsa
  4. Avocado, squeeze of lime
  5. Cheese to finish
~6oz protein + beans → 50–60g protein

The building blocks

Keep two or three proteins active at a time and rotate. Marinated and frozen are fully in play here — a real marinated steak or a bag of frozen roasted corn is still real food, and the whole point is cutting steps, not purity-testing the fridge.

03

Protein without counting anything

The stack you're actually hitting

5–6oz protein ≈ 35–45g + ½ cup black beans ≈ 7–8g + cheese or yogurt ≈ 5–10g = 50–60g in one sitting

Two of these a day plus your existing shake and yogurt snack clears your ~138g target without ever opening an app. Build the bowl version when you want the beans in — it's the easiest way to stack an extra 10g without extra cooking.

Why this works built around Trader Joe's

The carnitas and picadillo are fully cooked, and the carne asada, al pastor, and chili lime thighs arrive already marinated — the flavor work is done before you ever touch a pan. Real protein, real marinade, no spice rack required. That's not a compromise on the healthy-eating front; it's just where the convenience happens to live.
